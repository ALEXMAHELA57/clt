About Us

A pioneering Tanzanian organization at the intersection of resources, resilience and livelihoods.

Who We Are

A closer look at Carbon Link Tanzania

Carbon Link Tanzania (CLT) is a non-profit organization dedicated to addressing Tanzania's environmental and livelihood challenges. Through training, research and impactful projects, CLT promotes sustainable resource management, strengthens occupational health and safety, advances public health, and raises awareness of carbon trading as a route to foreign currency for local communities.

As Tanzania works to reduce poverty and build resilience to climate change, gaps remain in research on ecosystem integrity, sustainable waste management, and the use of digital tools to optimize natural resource use and public health. CLT was established to close those gaps — advancing resource assessment, sustainable management practice, and livelihood improvement through collaboration.

Mission, Vision & Goal

What drives our work

Mission

To become a center of excellence promoting knowledge, skills and attitude for resource management across development initiatives, in service of sustainable, livelihood-focused development.

Vision

Managing resources and human health to enhance sustainable resource utilization, improve livelihoods, and build climate-adaptive capacity.

Goal

Efficient management of available resources toward human development — achieving environmental sustainability, livelihood improvement and resilience to climate change.

Where We Work

Nationwide coverage

CLT operates across mainland Tanzania, with active programming concentrated in the Southern Highlands — including Iringa, Mbeya, Morogoro and Ruvuma regions.

Objectives
  • Build technical knowledge and skills for resource management
  • Link projects, research and ecosystem actors through digital platforms
  • Promote efficient, sustainable waste management
  • Safeguard worker and community health & safety
  • Connect communities to the global voluntary carbon market
  • Advance community health education and disease prevention
  • Expand access to clean water, sanitation and hygiene
